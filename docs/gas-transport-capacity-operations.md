## Scope and versions

The first consumer is a manually declared commercial transport entitlement with
one exact native quantity basis, owning book/legal entity/counterparty versions,
service interval, eligible paths and dated capacities. It is independent from
physical pipeline asset limits and financial booking. A later financial contract
can reference its exact version; configuring an entitlement does not create a
Trade, physical flow, titled stock or cash.

The first entitlement identity retains its owning book/parties, reference bundle,
quantity basis, service interval and eligible path identities immutably. Capacity
revisions change only the complete dated limits under those retained identities.
Changing scope geometry, reference basis or legal ownership requires a separately
qualified continuity operation; it cannot remap accepted claims by matching names.

Each direct-path version captures an exact pipeline and eligible receipt/delivery
points at the same reference revision and gas product. The first direct-path law
requires both points on that pipeline. It does not discover an interconnection
or imply a multi-segment, fuel, loss or transit model. Path identities and later
versions remain explicit, retaining earlier accepted plan references.

Capacity uses the managed `gas_transport_gross_uniform_capacity_v1` law. Agreement,
receipt, delivery and path limits all have complete dated coverage. Every law
selector is submitted explicitly. Claims use distinct explicit receipt/delivery
windows and quantities. Capacity assessment does not establish route conservation;
these are chosen operating plans, not measured or accepted transport performance.

## Chosen plans

A saved plan is a complete replacement of one identified plan's chosen claims.
It retains a business reference, exact entitlement version, previous revision,
effective time, evidence and source context. Plans have separate identities;
replacement affects only its own previous claims. All other current plans under
the same entitlement continue to consume the shared parent/point/path capacities.
An explicit empty-release operation releases that plan's claims without deleting
its history.

Preview reads the complete authorized entitlement and current claim heads,
replaces the selected plan in memory and evaluates the entire resulting scope.
Admission pins the exact capacity version and all relevant heads. A complete
replacement explicitly selects `complete_feasible_replacement_v1` and requires
a feasible managed assessment and an independently verified native witness. A
stale source, intervening plan or version change requires a new review. Concurrent
plans cannot spend the same remaining capacity. No pro rata allocation, route
choice, gross netting or quantity clipping is inferred.

Definition changes preserve accepted plans. Their current-state assessment can
therefore show a breach under the new capacity version; a configuration revision
does not silently alter earlier claims. New or replacement claims under that
policy must be feasible under the selected known and current capacity version. Historical views retain
their original versions, plan heads and results.

The separate selected `explicit_empty_release_v1` operation requires the exact
current predecessor and zero new claims. It may clear only that plan while
other plans still breach a decreased limit. Every remaining breach is retained
truthfully. This prevents a decrease from blocking independent cleanup; it does
not authorize new or partially rescheduled claims, clipping or reallocations.

For this installed admission law, a latest source-known definition or plan head
that is future-effective makes new configuration and plan admission unavailable
at the earlier business context. Refreshing the source generation cannot hide its claims or
free capacity. Select a business context in which every latest known head is
effective. Broader activation-boundary assessment requires a separate law.

## Context, authority and persistence

Operations use an explicit business date and current or exact retained knowledge
context independently of a pricing close. The initial read-clock contract is the
existing `facility_business_date_end_utc_v1`; it does not define a gas day or
convert the separately entered stage intervals. Display/input times use the
user's configured zone.

`ReadMarketData`, `ReadTrades` and `ReadGasOperations` authorize the complete
entitlement scope; `ConfigureGasServices` publishes its definitions, while
`RecordGasOperations` records chosen plans. Exact book/party/reference versions
must remain authorized. No owned-stock or facility-total permission is implied.
Conditional or required authority is captured before business-source resolution
and its original deadline survives every wait and final publication.

Use immutable relational definitions, dated limits, plans, claims and canonical
actor-bound request receipts with exact foreign keys. Supported managed operations
retain mandatory commit admissions in addition to native source and membership
seals. An exact retry recovers its first outcome; an identical older fact is a
no-op that never reinstates its old head. Changed facts append revisions under
new knowledge time. Core relationships do not reside only in JSON.
