# Shared lifecycle delivery contract

D394 selects the following implementation contract. This document records required
behavior, not a claim that every capability is implemented or production-qualified.

## Ownership and extension

Reusable Instrument/Side definitions own economics and supported behavior. Trades,
bindings and stable obligation occurrences own accepted lifecycle state. Owned
inventory, operator claims and loan-return obligations remain independently visible
holdings after their source trades complete. New compositions reuse qualified
capabilities; new economics requires a qualified managed handler.

The managed valuation boundary carries a typed trade or native-holding owner,
binding and book through unit scopes, recorded cash, shared scenarios, sensitivities,
accounting and empirical attribution. Independent holdings have no trade identity.
Native storage remains responsible for exact ownership foreign keys and admission.
Checks qualify equal identifiers across owner kinds, separate partial payments,
ordinary cash PV, shared connected pricing, mixed sensitivity and retained identities.
This foundation does not qualify the native gas ownership operations by itself.

Shared inspection, action discovery, pure planning and projection contracts compose
native typed operations. The application coordinates current authority, exact
proposal review, expected state/evidence, atomic persistence and idempotent first
outcomes. A scheduler invokes those same operations under explicitly selected
manual, review or automatic action policy. Actual and hypothetical evidence are
different types; projection has no persistence path.

Lifecycle sources retain their explicit business date independently of the UTC
date of their instant. The shared read admission proves it against the exact
context and frame. A command may pin one immutable source tuple through restored
read scopes; identical admission retains its first deadline, and another tuple
requires another operation. The transaction rechecks that source before commit.
The existing two-argument UTC-day profile and recorded-close calendar restrictions
retain their separate contracts; this source lane does not broaden close sessions.

## Financial boundaries

Operation eligibility uses exact configured half-open windows and cutoff-resolved
restriction facts. Trading, marking, dependency use and historical inspection are
different purposes. Missing calendar, milestone or policy is unavailable. Personal
display filters never remove holdings or change dependencies. Live browsing hides
inactive contracts initially; an explicit toggle reveals muted labelled rows.

Curve outputs and captured input membership are distinct. Live output selection
uses its versioned marking policy; strict retained requests and historical source
matrices keep their identities. Relationship-specific finality rules may consume
qualified final settlements after upstream trading ends. No inferred roll, skipped
required sequence member, fabricated final price or substituted price role applies.

Retaining exact selected inputs and capturing eligible live outputs are separate
authorized operations. A fresh live capture selects the chosen session's declared
roots at its business instant, preserves all source and frozen pins, and refuses a
required root with no eligible members. Both operations qualify the declared output
graph strictly; retained source windows are dependencies, not additional requested
live outputs. Qualified frozen shaping anchors remain usable outside the live
output window only under their exact native policy and certified final evidence.
Saved close identities and their captured membership are never rewritten by a
display preference or a later session configuration.
Both capture modes retain the original command and first response before source
frame resolution on replay. A same-fact save acknowledges its current command
without creating another financial close. Browser recovery retains that command
across reload; current authority is required again on every retry.

The first release requires futures margin/final settlement, listed futures options,
financial swaps and physical gas. Physical futures require a qualified gas delivery
adapter. Variation margin transfers value into cash obligations, resets settlement
basis and preserves outstanding market exposure. A date does not prove payment,
exercise, assignment, delivery, title transfer or repayment.

The managed futures planner retains a complete causal determination prefix.
Correcting an old settlement recomputes its successor closure and appends cash
differences; changed predecessor links require zero-cash restatements where needed.
An adopted policy survives its selection window for later source corrections.
Rounding values, their held-binding scope and implementation belong to the exact
adopted policy rather than an unrelated reference catalog. These pure planning
contracts do not establish native persistence or automation qualification.
The adopted policy explicitly selects its exact official settlement curve,
independently of the mark/execution curve. The instrument resolves actual facts
from exact captured native observations, checking series, source, finality,
occurrence, quotation grid and certified knowledge time. Source prices and fact
contents are not client command fields. Retained determinations preserve their
complete source evidence even when a same-price correction changes no cash.
The native source reader requests only the policy's exact occurrence windows.
It resolves correction ancestry at the bound effective date and certified
knowledge prefix before checking finality; a preliminary correction blocks its
older final. Multiple independent events and incomplete chains refuse. Publication
receipts supply source knowledge instants; row order and receipt audit times have
no selection meaning. Source reading, durable adoption and accepted determination
membership have separate native contracts.
The planner also requires retained history to name its exact trade/binding, book,
quantity, execution price/instant, complete adopted policy and certified reader
context. Each accepted determination retains its own knowledge/publication
coordinates separately from source knowledge. These must be visible and causal;
several steps in one atomic operation may share a publication and its single
certified knowledge instant. Recomputed cash
cannot validate history belonging to another holding or amended financial terms.
The native adoption reader must establish these relationships through exact
versioned keys; a managed history object is not a persistence or authorization
certificate by itself.
The instrument's adoption proposal derives its quantity and opening price/instant
from the exact booked execution and validates the complete selected policy and
official source. A proposal supplies no accepted publication or knowledge time.
In particular the reader proves accepted trading publication-prefix coverage
using native trading contexts and certified trading receipts, separately from
market observation-prefix coverage. An accepted timestamp alone is insufficient.

Native adoption must be unique by stable trade/binding and retain an exact origin
revision and its owner, quantity, execution and pricing references. Adoption checks
the current head under the publication fence; later visible metadata revisions
must prove their supported continuity with that retained origin. The first native
profile refuses changed pricing coordinates until a qualified repricing
transition exists, including changes introduced by the separate retained-market
row. A complete policy's occurrence membership must be sealed against later
appends; immutable individual rows alone do not seal a version.
Saving policy configuration and adopting an exact saved version are separate
operations. A no-change configuration save returns the existing version before
the user chooses the adoption reference; it cannot silently attach a trade to a
different version from the one reviewed.

Replay and same-fact acknowledgements require live current authority before and
after waits. Each new request retains its own first outcome without another
financial publication. Initial settlement basis comes from native economic
execution price/time, independently of booking audit and amendment timestamps.
After adoption commits, an accepted PV state requires a newly captured certified
trading context and its actual publication receipt. A preview context or a wall
clock timestamp cannot certify that new state.

Migration 0256 implements saved dated policies, sealed occurrence membership,
stable native adoption and an inception-only state reader locally. Adoption retains
the original holding revision separately from later metadata heads. Its basis uses
economic execution time; visibility starts at the holding revision's effective
instant. The managed reader verifies the exact booking date, party versions,
owner, quantity, execution, policy, pricing reference and certified context before
providing state to ordinary Instrument PV. Migration 0257 extends this locally
with sealed native determination runs and complete causal history. The current
accepted heads are selected before their exact source-prefix evidence is joined;
missing evidence refuses rather than selecting an older determination. The old
inception reader refuses once accepted margin is visible at the selected business
instant. Events before an adopted financial revision takes effect require a
qualified prior-quantity transition; they are refused, never silently skipped.

Each original or adjustment contribution has an independent shared
`TimedCashPayment` membership. Reusable currency/due definitions have no holding
business start; their native metadata uses the beginning-of-time marker while
each owned membership retains its actual occurrence instant and certified
publication. Reuse must not hide an earlier holding behind a later definition
start. Original cash survives corrections. Same-price source replacements retain
new causal determinations without inventing cash. Due time does not prove payment;
recorded partial cash reduces only its owned membership through the common cash
state reader. Terminal cash settlement removes open futures exposure while
unpaid final cash remains an ordinary holding with explicit discount inputs.

The managed action coordinator derives quantities, official prices and all
monetary operands internally. Its review hash pins the selected financial target
and explicit correction due terms. A changed official source invalidates review
even when aggregate variation is unchanged. An existing immutable financial
snapshot is acknowledged without a new financial publication or a reset of
current basis; the response distinguishes acknowledged and current revisions.

Each save, adopt or determine invocation captures its original authority deadline before receipt
or publication waits. The application owns the transaction and rechecks that
deadline, actor, current authority and retained references after managed work,
immediately before commit admission. Hosted invocations also pin the sign-in
session's expiry and revalidate that session in the native final admission.
A replay uses fresh invocation authority,
independently of its old receipt's historical deadline. The deferred database
trigger is supplemental: a SQL caller can force constraint checks early. These
checks establish owned commit admission, not a caller-proof guarantee about the
exact commit timestamp. An uncertain commit outcome is reconciled using the same
request ID in a fresh authorized invocation.
Standalone reviews retain their own original read-authority deadline through
source resolution, planning and result admission. A later grant cannot extend an
already-admitted review. Derived native timestamps preserve UTC microseconds
exactly; display formatting supplies no financial rounding.

The native adoption check uses ordinary synthetic bookings in owned disposable
PostgreSQL. It covers version selection, retries and same-fact receipts, sealed
membership, both publication-fence orders against amendments, effective/knowledge
visibility and native inception through common PV. Source capture, economic basis
and accepted publication clocks remain separate. The journal check also covers
same-price and value-changing correction closure, surviving cash, partial payment,
terminal PV without an open mark and an explicit future-cash discount factor.
Privileged synthetic context construction verifies refusal when the source
prefix cannot cover a latest accepted determination; it establishes no ordinary
capture-path failure. Whole-portfolio/worker integration, physical final delivery,
HTTP/agent adapters, user-facing policy editing and browser qualification remain
delivery gates; the local native increment does not qualify those capabilities.

The local portfolio adapter now offers an explicit native-margin remaining-value
convention, separate from execution P&L. It reads the accepted journal and all
surviving cash memberships, retains determination provenance, and uses ordinary
Instrument PV and connected scenarios. Pending cash selects the captured currency
mapping and exact-instant timing explicitly; missing adoption or required discount
inputs refuse. It records ledger coverage for these exact occurrences and does
not exclude failed native positions. Scoped portfolio replay is checked against
independently calculated margin/cash values. Economic horizon projection requires
the separately qualified complete opening event prefix.
Owned transaction admission retains read guards through composite report shaping
and savepoint restoration, then rechecks them before commit. Registrations cannot
escape their owned connection/transaction. The expired original grant with a fresh
successor fails even when a review is wrapped in another result object.

Exact-instant cash has a separately selected UTC-day discount convention. It
interpolates qualified dated discount factors with rational elapsed-microsecond
weights, then rounds the conditional payment/calculation ratio once. Evidence
retains both instants, the exact rate definition version, complete tenor geometry
and precisely the observations consumed by its brackets. Equal marks cannot erase
a required pillar. This captured-instant PV profile does not qualify frozen clock
rolls or additional rate-risk axes. Native journal wiring is qualified separately
from that rate interpolation profile.

Gas continuation qualifies owned titled lots, distinct signed operator claims/debts
and title-transferring commodity loans with surviving return obligations and fee

retain owned value; dispatch consumes identified compatible sources. Missing supply
cannot create negative owned stock or implicit procurement. Unmatched observations
remain truthful reconciliation evidence. One economic interest is valued once.

## Scheduled execution

The source-derived task contract is specified in
[jobs and workers](automation.md#source-derived-lifecycle-tasks). A saved task
selects one recorded close, native occurrence, exact trade/product and action
policy versions, execution knowledge policy and one-off wall-clock schedule.
Manual, review and automatic modes use the same shared product handlers. A task
has no permission to invent observed title, assignment or payment evidence.
Financial effects and its exact complete occurrence result publish atomically;
unknown acknowledgement recovery uses the native first outcome. Separate
scheduler/runtime identities preserve current owner/delegator/service capability
intersection and each attempt’s original authority bounds.

An automatic listed-option expiry may create its contractual strike-basis future.
That booking records the actual service principal, original job owner, exact task
run and source option revision. A private transaction-scoped permit admits only
the derived option-event operation, original book, event request and contractual
effective instant. The future, option event, booking fact, selected delivery-policy
adoption, shared command and complete task result publish together. Ordinary
service bookings remain refused even when the service has booking permission.
Checks exercise the real managed task processor, omitted terminal results, early
constraint draining, unrelated bookings, exact-operation impersonation and guarded
helper upgrades against disposable stock PostgreSQL.

A new command acknowledging an existing option event identifies the current
command and separately retains the original acknowledged operation. It appends no
event or generated future; previously returned command outcomes remain unchanged.

## Economic horizon

The risk profile selects one common next configured close and all financial
assumptions explicitly. Supported price paths are shock-then-hold and linear native
primitive quote levels over actual elapsed instants. Derived curves are rebuilt.
Source-known facts stay fixed; projected fixings freeze only within their own path
after their event. Causal prerequisites order same-instant transitions.

Prepare all transition-input and residual-value dependencies before sampling.
Projected inventory, linked futures, operator claims and returns use ordinary
Instrument/Side PV. Paid interval cash and unpaid residual claims are disjoint;
internal position transfers receive no additional gain.

Absolute economic P&L is horizon residual value plus period cash valued at the
horizon, less opening value. Cash is explicitly nominal or accumulated using the
selected currency curve. Currency totals remain separate without qualified FX.
Report the no-shock time/carry result and market-only diagnostic separately.
Historical and Monte Carlo methods fully revalue this measure; event-aware
parametric risk needs separate qualification. Failed holdings or paths invalidate
the complete distribution; they cannot be dropped or resampled.

## Delivery gates

1. Shared eligibility and inspection, live curve/date reads and inactive display.
2. Native action coordination and futures margin/final-settlement operations.
3. Gas ownership, operator/loan operations and independent portfolio valuation.
4. Pure lifecycle projection, both paths and economic full-revaluation risk.
5. Independent adversarial review, disposable native integration and browser checks.

The demo may be rebuilt completely. Replace incompatible contracts and synthetic
records without a legacy financial compatibility layer. Release requires a concrete
backup/reset manifest, actual qualification evidence and publication approval.
