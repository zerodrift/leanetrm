# Retained-title gas parking

Status: bounded profile implemented with disposable local managed, native,
upgrade, worker and application-browser qualification. Aggregate CI and release
qualification remain separate gates. Broader service laws and economic
continuation are not qualified by this increment.

`physical_gas_park_custody_v1` describes Park with customer title retained. It
does not relabel a title-transfer loan. Customer/provider perspective, one exact
point, quantity basis, currency, park window, return due and independently
directed fixed fee are explicit terms in one captured reference bundle.
Incompatible reference revisions require a separately supported mapping.

## Title, custody and physical activity

The customer retains its owned stock throughout custody. Acceptance reserves
explicitly selected lots without changing their title quantity or marked value.
The provider records foreign-owned custody quantity without receiving owned
inventory, a commodity asset or an invented offsetting financial liability.
Provider entry explicitly selects no owned account and needs no commodity mark.
The customer's separate stock retains its own native valuation inputs.

The installed policy prohibits use or sale of custodial gas. It declares loss
application unqualified and provides no raw loss-recording operation. Corrections
to supported custody observations retain evidence requiring reconciliation,
rather than producing a successful zero-liability calculation. A custody
acceptance or return does not infer an injection, withdrawal or pipeline flow.
Physical facility activity and contractual allocation use their own supported
operations and exact source links.

## Acceptance and returns

One full acceptance must occur at or after the selected park time and strictly
before return due. Customer lot allocations explicitly sum to the committed
quantity; provider allocations are explicitly empty. Customer reservations must
preserve availability for every already accepted source-known stock boundary,
including later uses. A positive balance at the acceptance instant alone does
not establish funding.

Returns identify the accepted custody and exact original lot quantities to
release. No automatic lot ordering or allocation applies. They occur strictly
after acceptance and use the explicitly retained nondecreasing accepted-return
timing law. Contractual lateness does not discharge custody or prevent recording
overdue returns. An earlier late-reported observation that would reconstruct
reservation history remains recordable but requires reconciliation before
financial application.

Native reservations have immutable full releases. A partial return therefore
releases the original reservation in full and creates its exact remainder on
the same lot at the same effective instant, under one atomic custody operation.
The typed lineage records returned and remaining quantities. A final return
creates no remainder. Generic reservation spending or release must not bypass
the accepted custody-return operation. Neither transition changes title quantity.
Reserve policy admission applies only to a touched lot receiving a positive
remainder. A full release requires the independent release policy and does not
require an absent or expired reserve policy merely because another lot remains.

## Observations and command identity

Trade-scoped agreement, custody-receipt and custody-return observations retain
their own source key, exact revision chain, observed quantity, effective instant,
evidence and publication lineage. They do not require a provider financial
account. Acceptance and returns pin exact observed facts and require matching
quantities. A return observation cannot be consumed again under a different
business reference or revision. Corrections retain raw history and expose
reconciliation required; accepted title, reservation and cash facts are not
silently rewritten.
Opaque source and return keys contain at most 128 Unicode scalar characters.
ASCII-space padding is canonicalized, control characters refuse, and other
characters retain their exact identity. Free evidence supports multiline text
up to 2,000 scalar characters under its separate text contract.

Reviewed manual operations retain explicit action PolicyId/Revision and validate
the selected known version and current policy head. Customer reserve/release
bindings provide additional independent authority. No provider account or
per-trade binding exists solely to satisfy an unrelated account-based action.
Preview, execution and outcome recovery retain the same selection and source.
Exact command retries recover their first actor-bound outcome; identical facts
under fresh requests are no-ops. Business and knowledge time remain independent.
The native source proof excludes only this operation's own derived reservation
and release identifiers. Other changed stock facts, including future-effective
uses and quantity revisions, require an explicitly refreshed source. Typed
parents and members must belong to their exact changed-true command receipt;
another agreement's valid reservation or receipt is not authority for this one.
An authenticated existing event can lie beyond the supplied business or
knowledge view. Review identifies that acknowledgement explicitly, retains the
selected view's balances and reports zero newly performed quantity, reservations
and fee cash. It does not refresh the source or manufacture an event inside it.

Retained inspection presents supported raw observation revisions separately from
accepted performance. A mismatched raw quantity can remain evidence while its
financial application refuses. Only backend-declared instant cells display in
the user's time zone; evidence text is not guessed to be a timestamp.

## Fee and valuation

The fixed fee is an absolute amount with an independent pay/receive direction.
A nonzero fee has an explicit due instant at or after park time; zero requires
an explicit not-applicable due. Terms must fit the existing ordinary cash
consumer at booking: absolute amount below 10^20 and due year 1901 through 2199.
No larger gas-fee domain is silently substituted.

Before acceptance, the explicitly selected conditional service plan assumes
acceptance at the later of earliest park time and evaluation time while the
window remains open. It values the fee independently of principal. After an
unaccepted window closes, plan value is unavailable, including a zero-fee
agreement; it does not imply acceptance or cancellation.

Accepted custody replaces the conditional fee with an ordinary independent
`TimedCashPayment` membership. Bank payments use the existing shared cash
operations and their existing authority. Returning custody does not pay a fee,
and unpaid fee cash survives the final return. Paid cash leaves remaining
marked value; that measure is not wealth including bank cash.
The completed custody workbench retains observation and fee-payment operations
while withholding another custody-performance form. Payment recording retains
the exact independent cash owner, allocation head, signed amount, observed time
and explicit cash policy. Read-only outcome recovery uses the original canonical
cash request even after its navigation frame is unavailable; absence creates no
payment. Bank payments require their existing `AmendTrade` authority, independently
of `RecordGasOperations` for custody observations and performance.

Customer stock is included once through its explicit native ownership policy.
Provider custody quantity never becomes a gas-price exposure by offsetting
invented assets and debts. Current-clock price valuation, generated closes,
cross-clock P&L and complete economic horizon continuation require separate
qualification. The unqualified continuation paths remain fenced
for every source-known custody agreement until qualified, including future
effective, zero-fee and completed-custody states.

A zero-fee provider agreement retains service value zero and visible physical
custody without inventing a cash membership. If it is the scope's only holding,
the existing whole-book risk consumer explicitly reports no financial holdings
to revalue. A mixed scope values its independent financial holdings normally.

## Authority and entry

Select this profile explicitly within Park and Loan. Retained holdings keep
their native custody identity; booking recovery retains the public family and
exact profile. The ticket captures the independent instrument terms before
requiring its booking ownership context.

Reads require market/trade and gas-operations authority. Customer stock adds
owned-holdings read authority; performance adds its corresponding record
authority. Provider custody and raw observations do not acquire those inventory
requirements. Setup and discovery preserve original optional access before
source resolution. An owned grant that begins during a wait cannot add customer
data, and a successor grant cannot renew an expired original read deadline.
Supported operations retain mandatory managed commit admissions in addition to
native immutable relationships and deferred integrity checks.
