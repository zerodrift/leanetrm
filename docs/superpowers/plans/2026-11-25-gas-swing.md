# Physical gas swing implementation plan

**Goal.** Value and operate daily physical quantity elections with monthly or
term limits, optional bounds and changed-day counts. Keep commercial facts
exact, use shared deterministic planning with storage, and price assessable
future choices under the explicitly selected one-factor model.

**Contracts.** The [approved quantity design](../specs/2026-11-24-quantity-rights-with-limits-design.md)
defines rights, limits, facts and forward-plan examples. The
[reconciled swing design](../specs/2026-11-25-gas-swing-design.md) defines
how the counted joint stochastic value, separate comparison plan, incomplete
coverage and resource qualification fit that contract.

**Status.** Published D454 at 748b7695 is the migration and compatibility
baseline. The earlier start gate and Tasks 1–8 below are the original build
instructions for the implemented bounded profile ending at e3cd1355. Their
old checkboxes, policy IDs, numerical expectations and scope limits are
historical. Task 9 onward governs the expanded implementation. The expanded
profile requires new tests, implementation, measurements and independent review.

## Constraints on remaining work

- Keep exact retained Instrument memberships, concrete Side PV contracts and one
  external Trade scale. Neither an election nor an intrinsic plan executes cash
  or creates a new trade.
- Preserve all published identities, financial results, authority and native
  replay, particularly D433, D445, D448, D450 and D454. Only the narrow
  unpublished expectations listed after Task 9 are superseded.
- Red first for each new behavior. Use independent enumeration, independent
  high-precision values, or a consumer's externally observable result. Retain
  signed local increments and inspect the entire staged change at each step.
- Run commands from this tree, with explicit pinned toolchains and isolated
  unmodified PostgreSQL through the normal preview/verification runners.
  Check host capacity and use only task-owned resources. Serialize builds and
  frontend asset creation against full preview/qualification runs.
- TypeScript presents backend quantities, reasons and state. Keep every route
  on the shared authorized calculation and write operations.
