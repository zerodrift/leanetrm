01 / The workspace

The deal and its detail.
In the same place.

Start with a proposed trade, inspect the result, and book it when the terms are ready.

01

Work the trade.

Capture futures and financial swaps in a shared blotter. Keep the counterparty, legal entity and booking record alongside the contract.

02

Price before booking.

Try a deal without creating a position. Inspect swap payments and discounting, or a futures entry price and its open exposure.

03

Keep the context.

Choose the market close. Review versioned inputs and retain the quantities, units and contract references used in a calculation.

03 / The exposure

Move the market.
Reprice the book.

See which dated market quotes affect your futures positions, then compare values under shared up and down scenarios.

The pilot reports futures execution P&L and open quote exposure. Complete settlement and variation-margin processing are still in development.

  1. 01

    Identify the inputs.

    Instruments declare the market factors they use, including explicit curve dependencies.

  2. 02

    Share the scenario.

    Apply the same market move across the selected book, so positions are compared on a consistent basis.

  3. 03

    Inspect the change.

    Apply position quantities and compare the base, up and down results for the selected book.
