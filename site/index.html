For the front office

Every deal, priced before it is booked.

Capture futures, listed options, swaps, PPAs and physical deals in one blotter. Try a trade against the market before it becomes a position.

  • Price before booking

    Preview a swap's payments and discounting, or a future's entry price and open exposure, without creating a position.

  • A blotter built for a desk

    Filters, pivots, saved views, charts and exports, with each trade's detail docked beside the grid.

  • Curves you can trace

    Build forwards, basis and hourly shapes from the prices you choose. A diagram shows which inputs built each price.

  • Volatility in view

    Fitted smiles, rich and cheap heat maps and a 3D surface for listed options on futures.

For risk managers

Stress the book before the market does.

Exposures come from repricing the book under shared market scenarios, so every position is compared on the same basis.

  • Delta and gamma by contract

    A ladder shows each shocked contract's exposure, by book and by measure.

  • VaR and expected shortfall, four ways

    Parametric, historical simulation, Monte Carlo and a PCA hybrid, each with its diagnostics on screen.

  • Stress on top of stress

    Layer temporary market stresses, then measure risk around the stressed market.

  • History as it was known

    Reopen a past business date at a chosen knowledge time. Corrections add revisions and never overwrite the record.

AI built in

AI does the legwork. You make the call.

An AI agent is a user in its own right here, with its own identity, only the access you grant it and the same operations the screens use.

  • Ask the page

    Ask about what is on screen. Each answer is pinned to the market date and knowledge time you are looking at.

  • Drafts, not actions

    The assistant fills tickets, sets up risk runs and prepares curve changes. Nothing is saved until you approve it.

  • Your choice of model

    Bring your own keys for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI or Meta models. Usage and cost are recorded for each one.

  • No side doors

    The assistant works through a closed list of the application's own operations. It has no SQL, no arbitrary web requests and no code execution.

Security and audit

Locked down by role. Auditable by design.

Controls sit in the database itself, not only in the screens, and every result can be traced to the inputs that produced it.

  • Roles enforced in the database

    PostgreSQL row-level security applies each person's roles to every read and write, whichever screen or agent asks.

  • Separate duties

    Editing market data and approving it are separate roles. Managing access is separate from trading.

  • People and agents apart

    People, services and AI agents sign in as distinct identities. An agent acts only under a grant a person gave it.

  • An audit trail for access

    Role changes, agent grants and revocations are written to an append-only audit.

  • Nothing overwritten

    Trades, prices and curves change by new revision. A correction never replaces the record it corrects.

  • Results that show their inputs

    Each valuation keeps the prices, units and model versions behind it, so a number can be explained later.
